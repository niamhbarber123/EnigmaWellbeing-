Move gently

Small movement can calm the mind.

Neck release (30s)
Slow circles, breathe out long.
Shoulder rolls (30s)
Up, back, down — soften.
Forward fold (45s)
Let your head be heavy.
Child’s pose (60s)
Breathe into your back.
Legs up the wall (2–5m)
Rest your nervous system.
Seated twist (45s each side)
Gentle, no forcing.
