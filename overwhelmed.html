Feeling overwhelmed

Let’s slow this down together.

🌿 1. Slow your breathing
Try 4 seconds in, 6 seconds out. Longer exhales calm your nervous system.
Start breathing
🖐 2. 5–4–3–2–1 grounding
Name:
5 things you can see
4 things you can feel
3 things you can hear
2 things you can smell
1 thing you can taste
🧠 3. Reduce the list
Ask yourself:
• What actually needs doing today?
• What can wait?
• What can be done imperfectly?
💧 4. Reset your body
Drink water. Drop your shoulders. Unclench your jaw. Take one slow breath.
🫶 5. Ask for support
You don’t have to manage everything alone.
